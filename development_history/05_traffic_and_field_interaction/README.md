Files in this stage are preserved with their original local filenames.

2. `Rival car simulation with overcut and undercut.py`
3. `Dirty air and overtaking difficulties.py`
4. `Rival Cars multiple and pit rejoin position.py`
5. `DRS Style addition.py`
